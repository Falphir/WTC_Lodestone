**Privacy: how WTC Network uses your data**
Run by <WHO RUNS IT>. Questions or requests: <HOW TO CONTACT, e.g. DM a staff member>.

**What we keep, and why**
• **Applications:** your Discord account, Minecraft username, age and answers. They're posted publicly in the applications channel so the community can see who's joining. We use them only to review your application.
• **Account link:** your Minecraft account ID and name, linked to your Discord ID, so we can whitelist you on our servers.
• **While you play:** servers report who's online. This is never stored.
• **Server logs:** the website and servers log IP addresses, kept up to 14 days for security.

**How long**
• Approved applications are deleted from our database after 1 day, and denied ones once the re-apply wait ends (<COOLDOWN> days). The public post stays in Discord unless you ask us to delete it.
• Your link and whitelist entry stay while you're a member. They're removed automatically if you leave this Discord server or lose the member role.
• Backups are kept for 14 days.

**Who else sees it**
Discord (where this all happens), Mojang (to check Minecraft usernames), Visage/mc-heads (to show your skin in Discord) and our hosting provider. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

**Your rights**
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Ask staff and we'll sort it within a month. You can also complain to the CNPD (cnpd.pt).
